PROJECTS
Full writeups, code and results for every project are at saudassist.up.railway.app/projects. Section titles below link straight to each collection.

Agentic AI · 2 projects​
 Two agents that decide for themselves how much work a question deserves. The first picks its own depth: answer immediately, run a single bounded
search, or launch a full research pass that reads five sources live and streams the findings back. The second runs on my portfolio site, choosing turn by turn
whether it already knows enough or needs to pull a project's full detail. Both solve the same problem, which is stopping an agent from spending time and
money on questions that never needed it.
RAG and Retrieval · 4 projects​
Four studies of one question: how do you get a language model to answer from your own documents and actually be right? I built five retrieval approaches
and ran them against identical questions side by side, which showed the most sophisticated method was not the best one and documented where two
popular techniques broke down. The other three cover what happens next: trimming retrieved text so the context budget stretches further, tying every
claim back to the passage it came from, and testing how retrieval quality holds up as the document collection grows.

Fine-Tuning and PEFT · 1 project​
Adapting a language model to a new task without paying for a full retrain. I implemented the lightweight adaptation method by hand rather than importing
a library, then ran it head to head against a full retrain on the same task, measuring what you actually give up: training time, storage per saved model, and
final quality.

Vision-Language and Multimodal · 2 projects​
Getting models to connect images and words. One searches a real photo library from a plain English description with no tagging, no training and no labels,
relying only on the shared meaning between a picture and a sentence. The other is a small image-captioning system built by hand: a vision model and a
language model, both frozen, joined by a translation layer trained from scratch so one can speak to the other.

Deep Learning · 4 projects​
Four builds spanning security, image generation, text and audio. The security project finds intrusions in network traffic with no labelled attacks to learn
from, reaching 0.95 ROC-AUC across 22,544 connections by learning what normal looks like and flagging what isn't. The generation project builds two
competing approaches from scratch and surfaces a real quirk in how one reports its own progress. The other two classify sentiment across 50,000 film
reviews, where a simpler baseline won, and recognise short spoken commands from raw audio.

Machine Learning · 6 projects​
Six applied problems, each ending in a decision rather than a leaderboard score. Customer churn across 7,032 accounts, where three models were
benchmarked and the simplest won on the evidence at 0.836 ROC-AUC. Fraud detection across 283,000 card transactions, where the textbook approach
produced a 95% false alarm rate until the evaluation metric was corrected. House prices to 0.924 R² from 216 engineered features, and sales demand
forecast six months ahead at 83.3% accuracy. Two are honest negative results: heart disease models that came back statistically tied, and a
recommendation engine that only narrowly beat a naive baseline. Both are written up that way.

Robotics and IoT · 6 projects​

running vision on-board at 95.6% detection accuracy. A face-recognition entry system deployed at a university campus with 536 logged events, and a
second that takes attendance from a live video stream. Alongside these, a home automation controller running entirely on its own hardware with no cloud
